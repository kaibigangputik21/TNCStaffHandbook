It establishes expectations, responsibilities, procedures, and standards that help maintain a safe, organized, and enjoyable community.

I. STAFF CORE VALUES

Every staff member represents the community. Staff members are expected to uphold the following core values.

  • Respect — Treat everyone with dignity.
  • Integrity — Always be honest and trustworthy.
  • Fairness — Avoid favoritism.
  • Responsibility — Own your actions and duties.
7
  • Teamwork — Work together and support one another.
  • Professionalism — Maintain a mature attitude.
  • Patience — Think before making decisions.
  • Accountability — Accept responsibility for mistakes.

A staff position is a responsibility, not a privilege to control others.

8

II. STAFF RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Monitor assigned channels.
  • Assist members who need help.
  • Respond to tickets.
  • Review reports.
  • Enforce server rules.
  • Prevent unnecessary conflicts.
9
  • Report serious incidents.
  • Participate in staff discussions.
  • Maintain confidentiality.
  • Support community activities.
  • Follow leadership instructions.

Use your position to serve the community, not yourself.

10

III. STAFF CONDUCT

Staff Members Must

  • Remain respectful.
  • Follow server rules.
  • Communicate professionally.
  • Remain neutral in disputes.
  • Admit mistakes.
  • Follow the chain of command.
11

Staff Members Must NOT

  • Abuse permissions.
  • Threaten members.
  • Punish out of personal anger.
  • Leak confidential information.
  • Manipulate decisions.
  • Harass other members.
12

IV. TICKET HANDLING

Ticket Procedure

  1. Read the ticket carefully.
  2. Understand the concern.
  3. Ask for necessary information.
  4. Investigate the situation.
13
  1. Provide a clear response.
  2. Escalate serious cases.
  3. Confirm the concern is addressed.
  4. Close the ticket properly.

Never ignore a ticket because you dislike the person requesting help.

14

V. REPORTS

Reports allow the staff team to identify violations and investigate incidents.

A Report Should Include

  • Discord Username and Discord ID.
  • Description of the incident.
  • Date and approximate time.
  • Relevant evidence such as screenshot.
15
  • Names of witnesses when applicable.
  • Actions already taken.
  • Additional relevant information.

Important

Staff must review reports objectively and avoid conclusions without sufficient evidence.

False or intentionally misleading reports may result in disciplinary action.

16

VI. PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES

Possible Actions

  • Verbal Warning — Minor or first-time violations.
  • Warning — Minor or second-time violations.
  • Mute / Timeout — Disruptive communication.
  • Kick — Necessary removal.
  • Temporary Ban — Serious violations, and can appeal
  • Permanently Ban — Serious violations and cannot appeal.
17

Consider

  • Severity
  • Previous offenses
  • Intent
  • Evidence
  • Community impact
  • Circumstances

Never punish someone because of a personal disagreement.

18

VII. STAFF CONFLICTS

Disagreements between staff members should be handled privately and professionally.

Staff Should

  • Discuss the issue calmly.
  • Avoid public arguments.
  • Listen to both sides.
  • Seek a reasonable solution.
19

If the conflict cannot be resolved, a higher-ranking staff member should be involved.


Staff disagreements must never be allowed to negatively affect the community.

Professional disagreement is better than personal hostility.

20

VIII. CONFIDENTIALITY

Staff members with TNC Staff role may have access to information that ordinary members cannot see.

Confidential Information

  • Staff discussions
  • Internal reports
  • Ticket conversations
  • Personal Informations
21
  • Private investigations
  • Internal decisions
  • Different Logs
  • Automod Logs
  • Moderation Logs
  • Messages Logs
  • Punishment Logs
  • TicketLogs

Staff members with TNC Staff Role must never intentionally share confidential information outside autorized staff channel even in Staff Co-Staff Channel.

Confidentiality is a responsibility, not an option.

22

IX. STAFF ACTIVITY

Being part of the staff team requires reasonable participation.

Staff Members Should

  • Remain reasonably active.
  • Respond to assigned duties.
  • Participate in staff discussions.
  • Assist members when available.
23

Staff members who expect to be inactive for an extended period should properly inform leadership.


Real-life responsibilities should also be respected. Staff activity requirements must remain reasonable.

Communication is better than unexplained absence.

XI. STAFF ACCOUNTABILITY

Every staff member is accountable for their actions.

Having a staff position does not place anyone above the rules.

Staff May Be Reviewed For

  • Permission abuse
  • Unprofessional behavior
  • Unfair punishments
  • Inactivity
27
  • Failure to follow procedures
  • Confidentiality violations
  • Repeated misconduct
  • Abuse of authority

Serious or repeated violations may result in demotion or removal from the staff team.

Authority comes with accountability.

28

XII. GOLDEN RULE

Treat Others the Way You Want to Be Treated.


Behind every username is a real person. Every member deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.


Before making a decision, ask yourself:

"Would I consider this fair if I were in their position?"
